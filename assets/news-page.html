Cold Wave Tightens Grip on North India

Northern India is witnessing a steady drop in temperatures as winter settles in earlier than many residents expected. Mornings across Rajasthan, Punjab, Haryana and Delhi-NCR have turned chilly, with dense fog reducing visibility on highways and delaying several trains. Weather officials have advised commuters to travel with caution during early morning hours and to keep headlights on low beam while driving through fog. Doctors have also urged senior citizens and young children to stay warm, avoid sudden exposure to cold winds and keep up with warm fluids and a balanced diet. Local markets have already seen a surge in demand for woollens, heaters and blankets, while roadside tea stalls are enjoying brisk business in the evenings.

New Metro Corridor Opens for Commuters

A newly built metro stretch has opened to the public, promising to cut daily travel time for thousands of office-goers and students. The corridor connects busy residential areas with major commercial hubs and is expected to reduce pressure on already crowded roads. Officials said the stations are equipped with lifts, escalators, tactile paths for the visually impaired and digital ticketing counters. Commuters can use smart cards and mobile QR tickets, which are expected to make boarding faster and more convenient. Residents living near the route welcomed the move, saying it will save both time and fuel. City planners believe that better public transport will also help in lowering vehicular pollution over the coming years. Feeder bus services are also being planned to link smaller colonies to the nearest stations, so that people do not have to depend on private vehicles for the last stretch of their journey.

Board Exam Preparations in Full Swing

With annual examinations only a few months away, students across the country have begun revising in full earnest. Schools are conducting extra classes, mock tests and doubt-clearing sessions to help learners feel more confident. Teachers advise students to make a realistic timetable, practise previous years' question papers and give equal attention to every subject. Experts also stress that proper sleep, light exercise and a healthy diet matter just as much as long study hours. Parents have been asked to create a calm environment at home and avoid putting unnecessary pressure on their children. Counsellors say that regular short breaks improve concentration and help in retaining what has been studied. Several schools have also set up helplines so that anxious students can talk to trained counsellors whenever they feel overwhelmed.

Digital Payments Continue to Grow

Digital payments have become a part of everyday life, from small vegetable vendors to large shopping malls. QR codes are now commonly seen at tea stalls, autorickshaws and neighbourhood stores, and many people prefer to pay through their phones instead of carrying cash. Banks and payment companies are running awareness drives to remind users to stay safe online. Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone, and always verify a payment request before approving it. Cyber experts add that people should avoid clicking on unknown links and must report suspicious messages immediately. With a little care, they say, digital payments can be both fast and secure for everyone, including first-time smartphone users in smaller towns and villages.

India Eyes Stronger Growth in Space, Sports and Sustainable Energy in the Year Ahead

Space Programme Sets Ambitious Goals

India's space agency continues to attract global attention with its steady record of low-cost and reliable launches. Scientists and engineers are working on upcoming missions that include satellite launches for communication, weather forecasting and earth observation. These satellites help farmers, fishermen and disaster-management teams by providing timely and accurate information. Young students are being encouraged to take up science and technology, with several schools organising visits to planetariums and science exhibitions. Experts say that growing private participation in the space sector will create new jobs and bring fresh ideas. Educational institutions have also started offering specialised courses in aerospace engineering to prepare the next generation of scientists. The country's growing reputation in this field is seen as a matter of pride, and many believe it will inspire millions of young minds to dream bigger.

Cricket Fever Grips the Nation Once Again

Cricket remains the most followed sport in the country, and every match brings crowds to televisions, mobile screens and local grounds. Fans are discussing team selection, batting form and bowling strategies in homes, offices and tea shops alike. Coaches say that consistent performance, fitness and teamwork are the keys to winning big tournaments. Academies in small towns are also producing talented youngsters who are getting chances at state and national levels. Women's cricket has seen a notable rise in popularity too, with packed stadiums and growing viewership. Experts believe that better facilities, fair selection and regular match practice will help young players shine. Other sports such as kabaddi, hockey, badminton and athletics are also seeing renewed interest, thanks to better coaching and wider media coverage across the country.

Health Tips to Stay Fit This Winter

Health experts say winter is a good time to build healthy habits, provided people take a few simple precautions. A morning walk, light yoga and stretching help keep the body active even when the weather is cold. Seasonal fruits and vegetables such as oranges, carrots, spinach and amla are rich in vitamins and help boost immunity. Drinking enough water is just as important in winter as in summer, even if you do not feel thirsty. Doctors also recommend washing hands regularly, getting enough sleep and visiting a physician if a cough or fever lasts more than a few days. People with breathing problems or joint pain are advised to keep themselves warm and avoid stepping out during peak fog hours. Small, consistent steps, they say, are the best way to stay healthy throughout the season.
